Babu abin da aka karɓa daga sararin sama a nan. Idan ya dace, shiri ɗaya yana ƙunshe da tashoshi da yawa a lokaci guda, kamar a ainihin band.

Hanyoyin aiki masu lokaci suna farawa a slot na UTC, kamar a sararin sama: idan ka danna KUNNA, shafin yana saita agogonsa daga ayyukan lokaci na jama'a (timeapi.io da time.now, tare da cloudflare.com a matsayin duba na kusan) yana nuna ƙirgen baya zuwa slot na gaba. Idan babu wanda ya amsa, yana amfani da agogon wannan na'urar ya faɗa maka.
