Lambobin yabo na qFT8

A cikin manhajar, ga masu sha'awar rediyo da masu saurare (SWL): taurari don salo, bandeji, yankunan DXCC da murabba'in grid da kake tuntuɓa ko ji, difloma da ke cika yayin ci gabanka, da nasarorin da za ka iya rabawa.

Manhajar tana ƙirƙirar takardar shaida da kanta, daga rajistar ka: babu abin nema, babu abin aikawa. Akwai shirye-shirye biyu: HAM (saduwar da aka rubuta) da SWL (tashoshin da ka ji).

Taurarin HAM: kowanne daga cikin hanyoyi 14 (FT8, LQ8, FT4, JS8, JT65, Q65, CW, SSB, FM, RTTY, PSK, SSTV, DV, JTTY) da kowanne daga cikin maƙallan mitoci 13 (160 m zuwa 70 cm) yana ba da taurari har 10, a tashoshi daban-daban 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500, 1000, 5000, 10000, 50000. DXCC yana ba da taurari har 10, daga 1 zuwa 300 na ƙasashe na yanzu (akwai 340), kuma murabba'in grid har 10, daga 1 zuwa 2000. Duk haɗin hanya da maƙalli da ka yi aiki da shi yana ƙara tauraro ɗaya: jimilla 472.

SWL: taurari iri ɗaya don tashoshin da kake ji, a rukunin hanyoyin masu son rediyo, a ayyuka (watsa shirye-shirye, siginar lokaci, beacon, teku, jirgin sama, yanayi), da kuma ƙasashen DXCC da murabba'in grid.

Ba a buƙatar tabbatarwa: kowane QSO a cikin log ɗin qFT8 naka yana ƙidayuwa, an tabbatar ko ba a tabbatar ba, har da waɗanda aka shigo da su. QSO naka yana ƙidayuwa a SWL ma, tare da kowace tasha da ka ji, kowace tasha sau ɗaya.

Samu difloma ɗinka

  1. Buɗe Settings: maɓallin gear na sandar ƙasa (a Android, iPhone da iPad shi ne maɓalli na dama-dama; a kwamfuta, gear ɗin da ke sandar ƙasa), ko shafin gear a cikin web remote.
  2. A can sama-sama a cikin Settings akwai maɓallai biyu, QSO da SWL, kowanne da tauraro da adadin taurarin da ka samu. Taɓa maɓallin QSO ko SWL (danna shi a kwamfuta) don buɗe difloma ɗinsa; yana cika kai tsaye yayin da kake yin rajista da saurare.
  3. Cikakken allo: taɓa difloma (danna shi a kwamfuta da a web remote). Matsewa da yatsu biyu ko ƙafar linzamin kwamfuta suna zuƙowa, ja yana motsa shi, taɓawa sau biyu tana sake nuna dukan shafin; maɓallin fita yana kusurwar sama ta dama (Esc ma yana rufe shi a kwamfuta).
  4. Raba: maɓallin raba, a ƙarshen dama na layin da ke saman difloma ko a kusurwar sama ta dama ta cikakken allo. A Android, iPhone da iPad yana buɗe takardar rabawa tare da difloma a matsayin hoto; kwamfuta tana adana shi a matsayin fayil na PNG; web remote yana sauke shi. Maɓallin harshen difloma yana zaɓar harshen da za a zana shi da shi.

Tambayoyi da amsoshi

Menene qFT8?

qFT8 shiri ne na rediyon mai son wanda ba a biya, don hanyoyin dijital na sigina mai rauni, Morse, teletype, hotuna da murya. Yana aiki a wayoyi da kwamfutar hannu na Android, iOS, Windows, macOS da Linux, kuma ana iya sarrafa shi daga kowane burauza. Ba shi da talla kuma baya buƙatar asusu. Luis Quesada Torres, HB9IPH ne ya rubuta shi, tare da na'urorin fassara da rubuta sigina nasa da aka rubuta daga tushe.

Me ya sa qFT8 kyakkyawan manhajar FT8 ne don Android?

qFT8 cikakkiyar tashar FT8 ce don Android 6.0 zuwa sama, a wayoyi da kwamfutar hannu: haɗa radiyo da kebul ɗin USB ɗaya, qFT8 zai gane shi ya kuma saita CAT da sauti da kansa, ba tare da kwamfuta ko akwatin mu'amala ba. Yana aikawa da karɓar FT8, FT4, FT2, LQ8, JS8, WSPR da sauransu, yana amsa CQ cikin cikakken AUTO, yana kiyaye agogo daidai daga intanet ko GPS, yana nuna kowace tasha a taswirar duniya kai tsaye, kuma yana rubuta kowane QSO a ayyukan da ka zaɓa. Yana ci gaba da aiki ko allo a kashe. Kyauta, babu talla, babu asusu.

Zan iya amfani da qFT8 a kwamfutar Windows, Mac ko Linux?

Eh. qFT8 yana aiki a Windows (64-bit da 32-bit, har ma daga Microsoft Store), a kowace Mac (Apple Silicon da Intel) da kuma a Linux (Intel/AMD da ARM, ciki har da Raspberry Pi), da irin hanyoyi, radiyo da fasaloli kamar na Android. Abubuwan da ake saukewa a wannan shafi kyauta ne.

Akwai manhajar qFT8 don iPhone da iPad?

qFT8 yana aiki kuma a iPhone da iPad (iOS). iPhone ko iPad ba zai iya sarrafa rediyo ta waya ba, don haka yana kunna aika siginar rediyon ta VOX na rediyon ko ta wata na'urar sauti (audio interface) da ke kunna aikawa daga sautin.

Waɗanne radiyo ne ke aiki da qFT8?

Samfuran radiyo 155 plug and play ne ta USB, ciki har da QRP Labs QMX, QMX+ da QDX, Icom IC-7300, IC-705 da IC-9700, da radiyo daga Yaesu, Kenwood, Elecraft, Xiegu, Lab599, GuoHe, FlexRadio da (tr)uSDX. Duk wani radiyo na daban yana aiki ta kebul na sauti na VOX ko DigiRig, kuma ana iya amfani da na'urorin karɓa na SDR kamar RTL-SDR don karɓa.

Zan iya amfani da QRP Labs QMX ko Icom IC-7300 da wayata?

Eh. Haɗa QMX, QMX+ ko IC-7300 da wayar Android ko kwamfutar hannu da kebul ɗin USB ɗaya: qFT8 zai sami radiyon, ya karanta ya kuma saita mitarsa ta CAT, ya aika ya karɓi sauti ta kebul ɗaya. Ba a buƙatar katin sauti ko ƙarin mu'amala. Haka ma yake da kwamfuta.

Waɗanne hanyoyi ne qFT8 ke tallafawa?

Tuntuɓar sigina mai rauni: FT8, FT4, FT2 da LQ8, tare da JT65, JT9, JT4, Q65, FST4, MSK144 da sauran yanayin LQ. Fitilu: WSPR da FST4W. Allon rubutu da hira: JS8, RTTY, PSK, Olivia, Contestia, MT63, MFSK, THOR, DominoEX, FSQ, IFKP, Hellschreiber da JTTY. Rediyo na gargajiya: CW, SSB, AM da FM. Muryar dijital: FreeDV da M17. Hotuna: SSTV da DRAW. Bayanai: packet radio da APRS, da bayanan DMR, D-STAR, Fusion da M17. Don saurare (SWL): AM, FM mai RDS, watsa shirye-shiryen DAB da DRM, tashoshin lokaci, faks na yanayi, NAVTEX da sauran siginar HF, jiragen sama (ADS-B) da jiragen ruwa (AIS), tauraron dan adam na yanayi, radiosonde, tauraron dan adam na masu son rediyo, pager da LoRa. Da yawa daga cikin sababbin yanayin an yi musu alamar gwaji. LQ8 hanya ce ta sigina mai rauni da HB9IPH ya ƙirƙira wadda ke kammala QSO, tare da locator da rahoton sigina, cikin saƙonni huɗu.

Shin qFT8 yana aiki da POTA, SOTA, gasa da littattafan rajista na kan layi?

Eh. Yana nuna nassoshi da tallace-tallacen kai tsaye na POTA da SOTA a taswira da cikin jerin tashoshi, yana ƙirga QSO na ayyukanka, yana fitar da ADIF; WWFF, IOTA, HEMA, GMA da sauran shirye-shiryen lambar yabo, da gasa kamar ARRL Field Day, na gwaji ne. Ana iya aika kowane QSO zuwa QRZ, Logbook of the World, eQSL, Club Log, HRDLog, HamQTH, HAMLOG.online, Wavelog ko Cloudlog yayin da kake rubuta shi, da kuma zuwa shirye-shiryen rajista a cibiyar sadarwarka.

Zan iya amfani da qFT8 don saurare kawai (SWL)?

Eh. qFT8 yana karɓa kuma yana fassara kowane yanayi da ya sani ta hanyar mai karɓa na SDR (dongle na RTL-SDR a wayar Android ko kwamfutar Linux, sabar RTL-SDR a hanyar sadarwa, KiwiSDR naka ko na jama'a, da sauran SDR na hanyar sadarwa) ko ta hanyar rediyo na yau da kullum a yanayin SWL, inda ba ya aikawa ko kaɗan. QSO da ka ji tsakanin wasu tashoshi suna shiga rajistar saurare wadda ke fitar da fayilolin SWL ADIF, CSV, SOTA SWL da RAZA SWL. Sashen SWL na littafin jagora yana bayyana masu karɓa da direbobinsu.
